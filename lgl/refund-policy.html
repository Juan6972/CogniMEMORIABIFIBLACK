Returns and Refunds

This page explains how to find assistance with a CogniClear order. It does not set or replace the seller's return and refund terms.

Before purchasing

Review the seller's return conditions, eligibility requirements, time limits and any return shipping costs at checkout. If these are unclear, ask the seller before completing payment.

For an existing order

Use the support contact or order-management link in your purchase confirmation email or receipt to request a return or refund. Include your order number and the email address used for the purchase.

Ask support for return instructions before shipping any items. The terms applicable to your purchase and your statutory rights govern your request.

Do not submit another purchase to request assistance with an existing order.
